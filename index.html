What it does

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for speed, privacy, and accessibility.

AI Vision
Point your camera and ask anything. The AI sees and responds in seconds — all on your device.
Voice Input
Speak naturally, get answers spoken back. Full hands-free experience with TTS support.
Text Recognition
Capture any text in the real world — signs, documents, labels — and have it read aloud instantly.
100% Private
Zero data leaves your phone. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your conversations stay yours.
Fast Responses
Optimised for mobile CPUs using quantised Gemma model. Responses start in under 10 seconds.
Accessibility First
Built for blind and visually impaired users. Full TalkBack support, controller layout included.

Simple setup

Up and running
in minutes

No coding needed. Just download, install, and go.

01
Download the APK
Grab the latest release from GitHub. No Play Store needed — direct install.
02
Sign in to HuggingFace
Free account required. SuperVision uses OAuth to securely download the Gemma model.
03
Download the model
The 3GB Gemma model downloads once and lives on your device — never downloaded again.
04
Start chatting
Type, speak, or point your camera. SuperVision is ready to help — offline, forever.
